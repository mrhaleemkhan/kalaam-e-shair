Privacy Policy

This policy explains what our private publishing tool does with data when it connects to TikTok and other platforms.

1. What we collect

Only for our own accounts: the login token TikTok gives us after we allow access, our account's ID (open_id) and display name. We do not collect any data about viewers or other TikTok users.

2. How we use it

Only to upload our own videos to our own account and to check whether an upload has been posted.

3. Where it is kept

On our own computer, in protected files. It is never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than the platform itself (TikTok).

4. How long

Until we disconnect the account. TikTok tokens expire by themselves if they are not renewed.

5. Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → Kalaam-e-Shair → Remove.

6. Viewers

This website uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics.

7. Contact

Questions: message us on YouTube at @kalaam-e-shair.
