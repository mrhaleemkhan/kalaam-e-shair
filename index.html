About

Kalaam-e-Shair shares short videos of classic Urdu poetry: one famous couplet (sher) at a time by Allama Iqbal, Mirza Ghalib, Mir Taqi Mir and other great poets, recited with its meaning in Urdu and Hindi. All poetry we use is in the public domain.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@kalaam-e-shair

Our publishing tool

We use a small private tool to upload our own videos to our own TikTok account. Each video arrives in our TikTok inbox as a draft, and we review and post it ourselves. The tool is not offered to the public and is used only for the Kalaam-e-Shair accounts.
